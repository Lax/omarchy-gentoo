This manual covers the **Gentoo overlay only** — installing and living
with Omarchy's packages under Portage. For using the Omarchy desktop
itself (hotkeys, the AI tooling), read
[the official manual](https://omarchy.org/manual/) — it applies on Gentoo
too. The Gentoo side of themes and plugins is covered here, in
[Themes](themes/) and [Plugins](plugins/): their commands are upstream's,
unmodified.

1. [About](about/) — what this project is, and what it is not.
2. [Install](install/) — add the overlay, emerge the desktop, keep it current.
3. [Packages](packages/) — shipped, skipped (with cause), and the open queue.
4. [Differences](differences/) — the Arch→Gentoo deltas that matter day to day.
5. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting/) — keywords, licenses, USE conflicts, slow builds.
6. [FAQ](faq/) — the short answers.
7. [Themes](themes/) — the same commands as upstream; what ships; what's pending.
8. [Plugins](plugins/) — shell plugins work as upstream; elephant providers are packages.
9. [Developing](developing/) — the automations, the gates, and how to port a package.

News about the overlay itself lives in [news](../news/).
